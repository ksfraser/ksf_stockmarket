# Lead-Response Pipeline — Universal Intent Signals

## Signals That Work Across Any Business Type

These intent signals apply whether the pipeline is scanning for insurance leads, pipe band recruits, service customers, or event attendees. Replace the examples with your business's domain terms.

### Tier 1 — High Intent (Score 5-6)

| Signal | Example (Financial) | Example (Recruitment) | Example (Services) |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| Explicit seeker | "looking for insurance advisor" | "want to learn bagpipes" | "need a plumber" |
| Active shopping | "getting quotes now", "renewal shock" | "looking to join a band" | "shopping for quotes" |
| How-to/start | "how do I start investing" | "how to start bagpipes" | "how much does X cost" |
| Recommendation ask | "who do you recommend" | "any band recommendations" | "any good X in Calgary" |
| Fresh + specific | Posted <14 days, named city, clear need | Posted <14 days, named city, wants to join | Posted <14 days, named city, clear problem |

### Tier 2 — Moderate Intent (Score 3-4)

| Signal | Example (Financial) | Example (Recruitment) | Example (Services) |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------------|--------------------|
| Implicit need | "rates went up", "confused about coverage" | "thinking about learning pipes" | "anyone dealt with X" |
| General inquiry | "how does insurance work" | "what's a pipe band like" | "what's included in X" |
| Event/opportunity | "any good financial events" | "highland games coming up" | "open house near me" |
| Community/directory | "list of advisors in Alberta" | "pipe bands in Calgary area" | "plumbers in Edmonton" |

### Tier 3 — Low Intent / Market Intel (Score 1-2)

| Signal | Example | Use |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Already converted | "I switched to X, here's my review" | Referral source, competitor intel |
| Old post (>90 days) | Any post from 6+ months ago | Note but don't pursue |
| General discussion | "bagpipes are loud lol" | Not a lead, but market sentiment |
| Competitor activity | "CDPB is recruiting" | Competitive intelligence, not a lead |
| Broad directory | "list of Alberta tutors" | Market sizing, not a specific lead |

## Location Specificity Tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Score Impact |
|------|----------|-------------|
| High | Named city + province (e.g., "Airdrie, AB") | +2 |
| Medium | Province only (e.g., "Alberta") | +1 |
| Low | Canada-wide or unspecified | 0 |

For local service businesses (pipe bands, tradespeople, local advisors), location specificity is often the single biggest scoring factor. A post from the right city with weak intent may still be worth pursuing over a strong-intent post from the wrong province.

## Freshness Tiers

| Age | Score Impact | Action |
|-----|-------------|--------|
| < 7 days | +2 | Immediate outreach |
| 7-14 days | +1 | Outreach within 48h |
| 15-30 days | +0 (baseline) | Normal scoring |
| 31-90 days | -1 | Only if no newer lead covers same need |
| > 90 days | -2 or discard | Archive unless exceptionally high intent |

## Multi-Product / Lifetime Value Signals

### Financial/Insurance
- New immigrant → auto → home → life → business (5-product pipeline)
- New homeowner → home insurance → auto bundling → mortgage protection
- Inheritance/estate → wealth management → estate planning
- Small business owner → business insurance → key person → succession

### Recruitment (Pipe Band / Club / Team)
- Youth player aging out of junior program → natural adult band entry
- Family member of existing member → multi-member household
- Dancer/highland dancer → often interested in piping/drumming too
- Musician from other genre → transferable skills, may want new challenge
- Scottish heritage seeker → emotional connection, long-term membership

### Services (Trade / Professional / Local)
- New home owner → multiple service needs (maintenance, renovation, services)
- New mover to area → establishing all local service relationships
- Event organizer → recurring need, referral potential
- Referral from existing client → pre-qualified

## Trust & Credibility Signals

These signals appear across all business types and affect how you respond:

| Signal | Meaning | Response Approach |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| Throwaway account | Privacy concern, may be embarrassed | Respect privacy, don't push for identity |
| "Are you a scam?" / "pyramid scheme?" | Trust deficit, has been burned | Lead with credentials, transparency, third-party verification |
| "I've tried everything" | Frustration, high motivation | Empathy first, then solution |
| "Never done this before" | Beginner, needs hand-holding | Educational, no-jargon, step-by-step |
| "My friend recommended I ask here" | Warm referral, social proof matters | Acknowledge the referral, be helpful to both |
| Specific budget mentioned | Serious, ready to transact | Match to appropriate option, don't upsell unnecessarily |
