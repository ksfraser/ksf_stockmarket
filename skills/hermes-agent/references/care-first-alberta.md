# Care-First Auto Insurance — Alberta Market Development

## Overview

The Alberta government has finalized regulations for its **Care-First auto insurance model**, launching **January 1, 2027**. This is the most significant auto insurance reform in Alberta in decades and a major market disruption that KSFII should actively leverage.

**Key source:** Insurance Bureau of Canada press release, August 17, 2026 — "Care-First Nears Launch as Alberta Finalizes Key Insurance Rules"

## What Care-First Provides

- **Highest health care benefits and accessibility support in Canada** — care led by health professionals, not insurers
- **Highest wage and income replacement benefits in Canada**
- **Among the highest lump-sum payments in Canada** — up to ~$296,000 for serious injuries
- **Right to sue** for out-of-pocket recovery expenses not included in coverage, and for collisions involving Criminal Code or serious Traffic Safety Act violations
- **Average savings of $260/year per driver** starting January 1, 2027 (per IBC)

## Market Context

- **50%+ of Alberta auto premiums** today go toward litigation and injury claim settlement costs (per General Insurance Statistical Agency / GISA)
- **Multiple carriers have already exited Alberta** — cannot charge high enough premiums to justify staying
- **Financial Statutes Amendment Act, 2026 (Bill 27)** received royal assent on May 14, 2026 — legislative framework finalized
- Care-First redirects litigation costs to improve care and benefits while reducing what drivers pay

## Why This Matters for KSFII

1. **Major conversation starter** — consumers are aware and asking questions. "What is Care-First?" and "how will this affect my insurance?" are high-intent educational leads.
2. **Transition period creates confusion** — brokers who educate win trust. Position as the knowledgeable guide.
3. **Carriers exiting = fewer options** — broker navigation value increases as the carrier pool shrinks.
4. **Renewal wave near Jan 1, 2027** — consumers will have questions during renewal season. Pre-position educational content.
5. **Credible third-party reference** — IBC's "strongest coverage in Canada" messaging is a trustworthy anchor for consumer conversations.

## Recommended Approach

- **Proactively educate existing clients** on how Care-First affects their coverage and whether it saves them money
- **Monitor for Care-First questions** on Reddit (r/alberta, r/Edmonton, r/Calgary, r/PersonalFinanceCanada) — these are leads asking for expertise
- **Use IBC messaging as a reference point** — it's consumer-facing, credible, and from a recognized industry body
- **Bundle with price shock conversations** — many Alberta drivers are already frustrated with premiums; Care-First offers a forward-looking solution narrative
