Your New Year-end Account Report
Investment account reporting in Canada is changing. New reporting regulations will help
you get a clearer picture of how your investments are doing and what they cost you. Once a
year, you will receive a Year–end Account Report for each account you hold with CIBC
Investor's Edge. It will contain information which you can use to assess your progress
towards your investment goals.
Account performance
The Year-end Account Report shows the change in your account considering deposits and
withdrawals in addition to the change in value. This measure of performance is called the
individual rate of return and is unique to you. It will be a useful tool to help you see if you
are on track to meeting your goals.
Account fees
The Year-end Account Report also summarizes all fees related to your account that CIBC
Investor's Edge earned during the year. It clearly identifies fees we received from you and
any fees we may have received from others related to investment products and services
provided to you over the year. We value having you as a client, and we hope you value the
service we provide.
