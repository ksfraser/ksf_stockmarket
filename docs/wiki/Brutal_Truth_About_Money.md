# Brutal Truth About Money

## Patterns of Wealthy People

### Owners

We train kids to be consumers, not owners.

**iPhone example — 18 years**
If you had purchased shares with the money you spent on new iPhones every time one came out, you’d have roughly **$326,000 worth of shares** today.

### Focused on Not Losing Money

Poor people are focused on making money.
Wealthy people are focused on preserving and growing what they already have.

**Why losing hurts more than winning helps**
If you lose 50% of a $100,000 investment, you need to make **100%** just to break even.

### Asymmetrical Risk-Reward

Smallest risk with most upside.
If we are going to risk a dollar, we want to be able to make five.

### Smartest Investors Are Usually the Worst

The smartest investors often struggle because they want the most information before acting.
By the time they have all the data, the opportunity is gone.

### Diversification

Find **8–12 uncorrelated investments** you feel strongly about.
You reduce your risk by ~80% and enhance your upside.

- Stocks and bonds are generally uncorrelated.
- Private equity and private real estate add further diversification.
- Over 39 years, private equity returned **15%** on average, while the S&P returned **9%**.
